I work on embodied AI and robotics, connecting vision-language reasoning with spatial representations and robot action. My research spans explicit 3D reasoning and memory-conditioned world models for geometrically consistent, camera-controlled video generation.

Open to Research Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer opportunities in embodied AI and robotics in the San Francisco Bay Area. Expected graduation: May 2027.

I received my M.S.E. from JHU in 2024, advised by Prof. Zongwei Zhou, and my bachelor's degree from Wuhan University in 2022, advised by Prof. Deng-Ping Fan. Previously, I interned at Philips Research, Microsoft Research Asia, Tencent WeSure, and Kuaishou, working on generative data augmentation, causal modeling, and applied machine learning.

VFlash: Fast, Accurate System-1 Visual Planning for Physical AI

Flash Primitive Decoding predicts complete visual primitives in parallel, improving geometric accuracy beyond its autoregressive target. It reduces 3D robot trajectory ADE by 42.6% on average, accelerates robot inference by up to 5.2×, and raises MolmoSpaces manipulation success from 62.6% to 70.2%.

Yu-Cheng Chou*, Shihao Wang*, Jin Huang*, Chi-Pin Huang, Chiao-An Yang, Xianzhe Fan, Jing Wang, Zhiqi Li, Yuanguo Kuang, Ruijie Zheng, Johan Bjorck, Linxi “Jim” Fan, Sifei Liu, Hang Su, Abhishek Badki, Lei Zhang, Alan Yuille, Jan Kautz, Zhiding Yu
Project
Captain Safari: A World Engine with Pose-Aligned 3D Memory

Pose-aligned 3D memory grounds camera-controlled video generation in observed scene geometry, preserving consistency under strong parallax and aggressive 6-DoF motion. Fixed-size world tokens keep denoising conditioning cost independent of memory length. The model earned 67.3% of 1,500 votes in a five-way human preference study.

Yu-Cheng Chou, Xingrui Wang, Yitong Li, Jiahao Wang, Cihang Xie, Alan L Yuille, Junfei Xiao

SpatialReasoner: Towards Explicit and Generalizable 3D Spatial Reasoning

Explicit 3D locations and orientations connect perception, geometric computation, and compositional reasoning in VLMs. SFT-to-GRPO post-training improves generalization to unseen spatial questions, reaching 60.3% accuracy on 3DSRBench with a 7B model and outperforming Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking by 9.2%.

Wufei Ma*, Yu-Cheng Chou*, Qihao Liu*, Xingrui Wang, Celso de Melo, Jieneng Chen, Jianwen Xie, Alan Yuille
